# @aamir.edits.pro

**Platforms:** Instagram (@aamir.edits.pro / @aamir.abba_)
**Niche:** Short-form video editor showcasing client work via Before/After transformations
**Why Manny saves them:** The Before/After card format is a clean, credible way to show transformation — directly applicable to property content (before/after suburb stats, before/after market conditions, before/after buyer situation).

## Signature vibe
Minimal, proof-driven. The entire format is the Before/After stacked phone card — no face of his own, just client results. Dark canvas, rich ambient lighting on the "After" side, flat green-screen on the "Before". Let the work speak.
